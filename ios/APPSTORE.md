## Description

OathSteps helps you prepare for the U.S. naturalization interview with short daily practice.

PRACTICE THE RIGHT TEST
Enter your N-400 filing date and OathSteps picks your civics test: the 2008 test for filings before October 20, 2025, or the 2025 test for filings on or after that date. If you are 65 or older and have been a permanent resident for 20 years, you can choose the 65/20 question set.

RECALL FIRST
Each card asks before it tells. Answer aloud from memory, then check yourself against the official accepted answers. Questions you miss come back sooner. Questions you know come back later.

MOCK INTERVIEWS WITH THE REAL STOP RULES
Try a five-question sample walkthrough, or a full-format mock that stops when the real test would.

INTERVIEW ENGLISH
Read and write sample sentences, learn common officer instructions, and practice N-400 vocabulary and truthful answers about yourself.

TRACK YOUR JOURNEY
Enter your own milestone dates, follow a four-stage preparation guide with links to official pages, get reminders before appointments and add dates to your calendar.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account needed. Your progress stays on your iPhone. Speech recognition runs on your device. No ads and no tracking.

IMPORTANT
OathSteps is a private study tool. It is not affiliated with USCIS or any government agency. It does not give legal advice and does not decide eligibility. Questions and answers come from the official USCIS civics lists. They are machine-checked against the official documents and not yet reviewed by an expert. Some answers change after elections or depend on where you live, and the app tells you to check the official source.

## Notes for App Review

No account or sign-in is needed, and every feature works offline on the device. Accounts are switched off in this build.

To see a learner with history, tap "Explore with a demo learner's history" on the welcome screen. Demo data is labeled "Demo" until cleared in Settings.

The questions come from the public USCIS civics test lists, which are U.S. government works. OathSteps is not affiliated with USCIS and says so on the welcome screen, in Settings and in the description.

The microphone and speech recognition are requested only when the user chooses to answer aloud. Recognition runs on the device only and no audio leaves the iPhone. Notifications are local reminders the user turns on.
